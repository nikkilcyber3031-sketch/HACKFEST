What is HACKFEST?

HACKFEST is a hackathon that brings students and tech enthusiasts together to create innovative solutions, solve real-world problems, and showcase their technical skills through teamwork and creativity.


Participants team up with fellow creators, transform bold ideas into innovative projects and demonstrate their creativity, technical skills, collaboration and problem-solving ability.
